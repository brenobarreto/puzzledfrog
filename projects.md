## torg.dev

<a href="https://torg.dev" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">View Project →</a>

Torg is an RPG-based, AI-powered Computer Science learning platform. Learners move through a journey map of quests made of readings and missions that ask for real code or AI-graded system design.

### Features

- **Journey maps**: each journey is a map of quests, and every quest ends with a boss (a final trial).
- **Progression**: earn XP and crests as you complete quests, with an avatar and rank that grow with you.
- **Cards**: collectible cards you earn along the journey, which also work as quick review material.
- **Readings**: short, focused explanations of each concept, placed right before the practice that uses it.
- **Coding missions**: solve problems in Python, JavaScript or Java. Your code runs in a sandbox against real tests, with hints available if you get stuck.
- **System design missions**: design real systems on a canvas and get your work graded against a rubric.
- **AI-graded drills**: 200+ short questions answered in your own words, or in code. The AI judges each rubric item, tells you what's missing without giving away the answer, and gives you a second try. Spaced repetition brings questions back.
- **Book quests**: recommended readings with AI-powered discussions. The AI tutor talks through the material with you.
- **Company-driven journeys** (WIP): prep paths shaped around a specific company's interview style and the topics it tends to ask.

### Where you can use it

- **Website**: the full experience.
- **MCP**: use Torg from your own AI assistant (Claude, Codex, etc.).
- **CLI** (WIP): practice from the terminal.
- **Mobile app** (WIP): quick drills and reviews on the go, reusing the same API.
